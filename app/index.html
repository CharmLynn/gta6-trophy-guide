⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (bronze) — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. The first spot is offered at home in Chapter 1 and is gone after that chapter, and it is not confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it in Chapter 1 to be safe. PowerPyx and GTABase agree it is the only trophy that can be missed; story trophies and everything on the 100% checklist cannot be missed. Read its guide ↓

Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 at about 200 hours, in one playthrough: free roam after the story, no mission replay, no New Game+, no difficulty settings. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep trophy runs cheat-free (PowerPyx, GTABase).
